# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

- Initial standalone Synctick framework: deterministic sessions, verified startup,
  participant identity, recording, replay, and structured lifecycle management.
- Bevy 0.18 adapter with separate simulation workers and presentation publication.
- `Wire` and `StableHash` derives, including explicitly tagged enums and
  field-level `#[stable_hash(skip)]` for non-authoritative caches.
- Wallet and Power Garden examples, maintained in the repository.
- Apache-2.0 licensing with attribution in NOTICE.
